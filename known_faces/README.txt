Put one folder per person in here, named "Firstname Lastname".
Inside each folder put about 5 clear photos of that person's face (.jpg / .png).

Example:
  known_faces/
      Vivaan Kumar/
          1.jpg
          2.jpg
          3.jpg
          4.jpg
          5.jpg

The app greets them with the SECOND word of the folder name:
  "Vivaan Kumar"  ->  "Welcome, Mr. Kumar"

Photo tips: face clearly visible, good lighting, a few slightly different
angles (straight, a little left, a little right), no sunglasses.
iPhone .HEIC photos are not supported - export/convert them to .jpg first.

After adding photos, click "Reload Faces" in the app (or restart it).
Photos in this folder are NOT uploaded to GitHub (see .gitignore).
